The **differential threshold**, in psychophysics, is the minimum threshold of difference between two physical quantities to perceive a difference between the two. For instance, it's the minimum difference in weight that a person is capable of perceiving when given two objects to hold. A person is likely capable of telling the difference between a 100 gram and 90 gram object, but likely won't be between a 100 gram and 99 gram object.
