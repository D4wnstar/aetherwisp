**Fechner's law** is an empirical law in psychophysics that states that the intensity of a stimulus is logarithmically related to the intensity of the sensation. Formally,
$$S=c\log I=\frac{1}{\log(1+K)}\log I$$
where $S$ is the intensity of sensation, $I$ is the intensity of stimulus and $K$ is [[Weber's law|Weber's constant]]. $c$ is a convenience constant defined as $c=1/\log(1+K)$.
