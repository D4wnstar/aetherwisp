**Floating point operations per second** (**FLOPS**) is a measure of the performance of a computer, specifically with respect to mathematical operations on [[floating point number|floating point numbers]]. This measure depends on the [[computational number formats|format]] (bit size) of the floating points and any FLOPS value should report the what floats are used. A typical size is FP64.
## Top 500
As of 2026, the [Top 500](https://www.top500.org/) list is bi-yearly publication that reports the 500 most powerful machines in the world on the basis of their sustained FLOPS (in FP64), as measured through the High Performance Linpack code (HPL). In short, HPL solves a system of $n$ random linear equations, then reports the performance over the benchmark using the standard formula $\frac{2}{3}n^{3}+2n^{2}$.
