**High performance computing** (**HPC**) refers to computing systems with extremely high computational power that are able to solve hugely complex and demanding problems.

HPC actually refers to many interconnected elements. The most common ones to come to mind are hardware and specific programming expertise. HPC relies deeply on supercomputers with enormous computational power to the point that the two terms are almost synonymous. Moreover, HPC also relies on specific technical programming patterns intended to maximize how efficiently code runs on this hardware. But HPC has many, many more aspects: cloud computing and interconnection concerns, translating mathematical models to a form that is computable on a machine, economical and energy aspects of the compute clusters... in simple terms, HPC is a large field without a clear definition.
