**Stevens' law** is an empirical law in psychophysics that states that the intensity of a stimulus is related to the intensity of the sensation by a power law. It is a generalization of [[Fechner's law]]. Formally,
$$S=mI^{n}$$
where $S$ is the intensity of sensation, $I$ is the intensity of stimulus and $m$ is a constant. $n$ is a real number interpreted as the relationship between stimulus and sensation.
