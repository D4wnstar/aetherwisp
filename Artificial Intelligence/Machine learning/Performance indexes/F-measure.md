The **F-measure**, also called **F-score**, **F1-score** or **F1**, is a [[performance index]] related to [[precision]] and [[recall]]. It is defined as
$$\text{F}=2 \frac{\text{Prec}\cdot\text{Rec}}{\text{Prec}+\text{Rec}}$$
It is the [[mean|harmonic mean]] of precision and recall. It is defined between 0 and 1.
