**Recall**, in the context of [[machine learning]], is a [[performance index]] of [[binary classification]] defined as the ratio between true positives and all positives:
$$\text{Rec}=\frac{\text{TP}}{\text{P}}=\text{TPR}$$
It is identical to [[False Positive Rate|TPR]].

The name difference is a historical artifact since recall comes from information retrieval, e.g. search engines, and is generally the preferred term in that domain.
