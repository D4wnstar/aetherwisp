The **mean squared error** or **mean squared deviation** of a [[sample]] is the [[mean]] of the squares of the errors:
$$\text{MSE}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N} (x_{i}-\hat{\mu})^{2}$$
where $x_{i}$ is the $i$-th sample, $\hat{\mu}$ is the sample mean and $N$ is the sample size. Alternatively, in the case of an [[estimator]] over the sample, it can also be written as
$$\text{MSE}=b^{2}+\hat{\sigma} ^{2}$$
where $b$ is the [[Estimator|bias]] of the estimator and $\hat{\sigma}^{2}$ is the [[Variance|sample variance]]. If the estimator is unbiased, it coincides with the sample [[Variance]].
