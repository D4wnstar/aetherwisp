A collection of [[Random variable|random variables]] $X_{1},\ldots,X_{N}$ is said to be a **random sample** or simply just a **sample** of size $N$ if they are [[iid]]. A sample is a finite subset of the total **population**; for instance, measurements ran on one-hundred people are a sample of the total human population.

The fundamental principle of statistics is that conclusions drawn from a sample are approximately representative of the properties of the total population.
